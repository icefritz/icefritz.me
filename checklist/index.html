Your progress

Auto-complete from AQW

Checks your real AQW inventory and bank for these items, using AQW's own inventory page. Nothing is sent anywhere but this page.

  1. Drag AQW sync to your bookmarks bar.
  2. Log in at account.aq.com
  3. On that page, click the AQW sync bookmark. This page opens, ticks what you own and fills in how many you have for your goals.
On a phone, or prefer copy and paste?

While logged in, open your AQW inventory data, select all, copy, and paste it here. A plain list of item names (one per line) works too.

Save, move or restore

Progress lives in this browser only. Export it to keep a backup or to carry it to another device, then import it there.
